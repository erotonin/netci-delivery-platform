# Code of Conduct

netCI is built in a professional, collaborative environment. Participants must
be respectful, assume good faith, discuss technical decisions with evidence,
and make space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily suspend
or permanently exclude participants whose behaviour harms the community. To
report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through the private
contact route described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled discreetly,
fairly and without retaliation.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events and
other spaces where a person is participating as a member of the netCI project.
